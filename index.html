Good to know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?+
All of Puget Sound: Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Everett, Kitsap and surrounding communities. Travel beyond 30 miles adds a small fee.
How soon will I get my photos?+
Standard delivery is about one week via a private online gallery. A 48-hour rush is available for an added fee.
Do I get print rights?+
Yes, every package includes a print release so you can print your images wherever you like, or order through the gallery.
What should we wear?+
I'll send guidance once we book. The Signature and Full packages include wardrobe/location planning to make it easy.
