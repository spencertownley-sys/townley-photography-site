The best senior-portrait spots in Seattle

Every September I get some version of the same question: where should we shoot senior portraits? Here are five spots around Seattle that consistently deliver, in order of how forgiving they are of whatever time of day you can actually get on the calendar.

1. Kerry Park, early morning

The skyline shot everyone wants, but early is the trick, before the tour buses and the crowds, the light is softer and you're not fighting for space on the wall.

2. Discovery Park bluffs

Big sky, tall grass, water in the background. Best about an hour before sunset. Bring layers, it's exposed and the wind picks up.

3. Gas Works Park

Industrial texture plus skyline plus water. Good for a senior who wants something less "postcard" and more character.

4. The Arboretum

The one to book if it's cloudy, the tree canopy is forgiving in flat light and gives you a genuinely different look than the rest of this list.

5. Golden Gardens

Beach, driftwood, sunset over the water. The classic choice for a reason, just plan around tide charts if you want the wet-sand reflection shots.
