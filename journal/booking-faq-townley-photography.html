FAQ · July 2026

Frequently asked questions about booking a session

These are the questions I actually get asked before people book, answered in one place. Deposits, turnaround, travel, weather, print rights, and what happens after you hit send on the inquiry form.

If your question is not here, ask it directly; the form takes about a minute and I answer within one to two business days.

How do I book, and what happens next?

Start with the inquiry form: service, rough date, location, and anything useful. I reply within one to two business days with availability, options, and a quote. When we confirm, a 25 percent deposit holds your date and the rest is due around the session.

What does a session cost?

Portrait packages run $250 for a mini, $450 for the Signature, and $700 for the Full Experience, with what each includes listed on the portrait page. Real estate, event film, and marketing video are quoted per project, because scope varies too much for a menu to be honest.

How fast do I get my photos or video?

Photos are delivered within 48 hours through a private online gallery. Video projects run a one week turnaround. Both numbers are the standard, not a rush fee, and they are stated in the booking, not implied.

What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

A 25 percent deposit holds your date, refundable with one week of notice. Inside a week, the deposit covers the slot nobody else could book. When weather forces a reschedule, the deposit simply moves with the new date; living in the Puget Sound means the sky gets a vote.

What happens if it rains?

Drizzle is usually shootable and sometimes better than sun; overcast is soft, flattering light and I do not cancel for clouds. Real rain moves the session, and I make that call with you a day or two ahead instead of gambling your afternoon. Nobody pays a penalty for the weather.

Do you travel?

I serve the greater Seattle and Tacoma area, and travel beyond 30 miles adds a small fee that I quote up front. If you are further out, ask anyway; the answer is usually yes with logistics.

Do I get print rights? How many photos?

Every portrait package includes a private gallery and a print release, so you can print your images wherever you like. Image counts by package are on the portrait page, and extra edited images beyond your package are $20 each.

Are you licensed for drone work?

Yes. I hold an FAA Part 107 certificate and handle airspace authorization for listings and properties that need aerials, which much of the Seattle area technically does.

What should we wear? Where should we shoot?

Both come with guidance after booking, wardrobe notes included, and location planning is part of every session. Green Lake, Kerry Park, and Lincoln Park are my go-to portrait spots, each best in a different season, and the Journal has full guides on wardrobe and locations if you want homework.
