How much do portrait photographers cost in Seattle? A real pricing breakdown

Portrait sessions in Seattle generally run from under $200 with a newer photographer to well over $1,000 at a high-end studio. My packages sit deliberately in the middle at $250, $450, and $700, and this post breaks down exactly what those numbers buy.

Photographers are often cagey about pricing, which helps nobody. Here is mine, in full.

The Seattle market, roughly

At the low end you will find hobbyists building a portfolio, often talented, sometimes inconsistent, usually slow to deliver. At the high end are luxury studios where the session is an event and the prints are furniture. Both are legitimate. The middle is where most families actually want to be: professional results, professional reliability, and a bill that does not require a family meeting.

My packages, line by line

The Mini, $250: thirty minutes at one location, ten edited digital images, a private online gallery, and a print release. Right for updated family photos, a quick headshot refresh, or testing whether your toddler tolerates a camera.

The Signature, $450: a full hour with one or two looks, thirty edited images, location guidance before the shoot, gallery and print release included. This is the package most families book, and the one I recommend if you are unsure.

The Full Experience, $700: two hours across multiple looks or locations, sixty edited images, a wardrobe consult, a $75 print credit, and priority scheduling. Built for milestone sessions where you want range, not just coverage.

What every package includes

Professional editing on every delivered image, a private gallery, a print release so you can print anywhere, and delivery within 48 hours. That last one surprises people. Fast turnaround is not a rush fee here; it is the standard. Worth noting: these are portrait prices. Weddings, newborn specialists, and commercial work run on different math, and comparing a portrait mini to a wedding quote will only confuse the spreadsheet.

The fine print, stated plainly

A 25 percent deposit holds your date, refundable with one week of notice. Travel beyond 30 miles adds a small fee. Extra edited images beyond your package are $20 each. There is nothing else hiding in the invoice.

Questions worth asking any photographer

Whoever you hire, ask three things before you book. How many edited images are included, and what does an extra one cost? How long is delivery, in writing? And do you get a print release, or will printing your own photos cost more later? The answers vary wildly across the Seattle market, and the cheapest session price is sometimes the most expensive way to end up with photos on your wall.

How to choose

Pick the session length that matches your group's patience, not your ambition. A relaxed thirty minutes beats a tense two hours every time, and relaxed is the whole method here.
